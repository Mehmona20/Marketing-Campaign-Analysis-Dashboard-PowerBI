# Marketing-Campaign-Analysis-Dashboard-PowerBI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and marketing campaign performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard transforms raw marketing data into meaningful business insights that help organizations evaluate campaign effectiveness, understand customer segments, and support data-driven marketing decisions.

## Business Problem

Marketing teams invest significant budgets in promotional campaigns, but it is often difficult to determine which campaigns generate the best customer response and which customer groups contribute the most value.

The business needed answers to the following questions:

- Which marketing campaign achieved the highest customer acceptance?
- Which customer segments generate the highest income?
- How does customer age influence spending behavior?
- Which marital status group represents the largest customer base?
- Does family size affect customer spending?
- How can future marketing campaigns be improved using customer insights?

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Evaluate the performance of five marketing campaigns.
- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Compare income across different education levels.
- Understand customer spending patterns.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query.

The following steps were performed:

- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing values in the Income column using the Median.
- Removed invalid values from the Marital Status column.
- Removed blank records.
- Trimmed and cleaned text columns.
- Corrected data types.
- Created Customer Age calculated column.
- Created Total Children calculated column.
- Created Total Spending measure.
- Verified data quality before visualization.

## Key Business Insights

- Campaign 4 achieved the highest customer acceptance, making it the most successful marketing campaign.
- Campaign 2 had the lowest customer acceptance and requires improvement.
- Customers with PhD and Master's education levels have the highest average income.
- Married customers represent the largest customer segment.
- Customers between approximately 50 and 60 years of age show higher spending behavior.
- Customers with fewer children tend to spend more on wine products than larger families.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

- Increase investment in marketing strategies similar to Campaign 4, as it achieved the highest customer acceptance.
- Review and redesign Campaign 2 to improve customer engagement.
- Focus premium marketing campaigns on high-income customer segments, particularly customers with Master's and PhD education.
- Develop personalized marketing campaigns based on customer demographics such as education level, marital status, and family size.
- Introduce loyalty programs and exclusive offers for high-spending customers to improve customer retention.
- Create family-focused promotional offers to encourage spending among larger households.
- Monitor customer purchasing behavior regularly to improve future marketing strategies and increase return on investment (ROI).
