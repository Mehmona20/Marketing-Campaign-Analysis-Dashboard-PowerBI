# Marketing-Campaign-Analysis-Dashboard-PowerBI
Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview

This project analyzes a marketing campaign dataset using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to evaluate campaign performance, understand customer demographics, identify spending behavior, and generate business insights that can support marketing decisions.

Business Problem

Marketing teams launch multiple campaigns, but it is difficult to determine which campaign performs best and which customer groups generate the highest value.

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

Which marketing campaign performed the best?
Which education group has the highest average income?
Which marital status group contains the most customers?
How does customer age affect spending?
How does family size influence spending?
Project Objectives
Analyze campaign performance.
Understand customer demographics.
Identify high-value customer segments.
Compare income across education levels.
Analyze spending behavior.
Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Dataset Information

Dataset: Marketing Campaign Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographic information, income, education, marital status, spending, campaign acceptance, and purchasing behavior.

Key Business Insights:
Campaign 4 achieved the highest customer acceptance.
Campaign 2 recorded the lowest acceptance.
Customers with PhD and Master's education have the highest average income.
Married customers represent the largest customer segment.
Customers aged around 50–60 show higher spending levels.
Customers with fewer children tend to spend more on wine products.
Recommendations
Allocate more marketing budget to campaigns similar to Campaign 4.
Improve or redesign Campaign 2.
Target customers with higher income for premium products.
Personalize campaigns based on education and marital status.
Focus loyalty programs on high-spending age groups.
